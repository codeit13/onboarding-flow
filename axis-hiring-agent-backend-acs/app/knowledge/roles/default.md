# Role Guide — General Banking Role (default)

If the question cannot be clearly mapped to a specific role, use the general Axis Bank policies and offer to connect the candidate to their Onboarding SPOC for role-specific details.

## Typical structure for any Axis Bank role
- Band range: AM to SVP. Your band is in the offer letter.
- Reporting manager: mentioned in the offer letter; introductions happen on Day 1.
- Mandatory e-learnings: Code of Conduct, PMLA, KYC, Information Security, POSH — all on Axis LMS, 14-day deadline.
- Buddy assigned before joining.
- First salary is pro-rated and credited on the last working day of the joining month.

## Who to ask for what
- **Offer, joining date, documents**: Onboarding Team — onboarding@axisbank.com
- **Salary, tax, FBP**: Payroll — payroll.query@axisbank.com
- **Benefits, leave, insurance**: HR Ops — hrops@axisbank.com
- **IT, laptop, email, SSO**: IT Helpdesk — ithelpdesk@axisbank.com
- **Anything unwritten**: your buddy.
