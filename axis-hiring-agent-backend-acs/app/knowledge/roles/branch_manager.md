# Role Guide — Branch Manager (BM / Flagship Branch)

## Reporting & Band
- **Band**: typically SM (Senior Manager) for a standard branch; CM for a Flagship branch.
- **Reports to**: Cluster Head → Circle Head (Retail Banking).
- **Team**: 6-18 direct reports depending on branch size (Relationship Managers, Personal Bankers, Service Managers, tellers).

## KRAs (measured quarterly)
1. **CASA book growth** — target NIM contribution from the branch's CASA + term deposits.
2. **Third-party product (TPP) revenue** — insurance, mutual funds, PMS referrals.
3. **Audit & compliance** — KYC completeness, AML alerts closure, RBI inspection readiness.
4. **Customer experience (NPS)** — branch NPS, complaint closure TAT.
5. **Team development** — attrition, succession readiness, L&D completion.

## Travel Expectations
- Predominantly in-branch. Expect 2-4 customer visits per week to top HNI / Burgundy Private clients — local conveyance reimbursed (see travel policy).
- Cluster-level reviews: once a quarter, overnight travel possible.
- No international travel in the BM mandate.

## Tools You'll Use Day-1
- **Finacle** (core banking) — already rolled out; training given in Week 1.
- **CRM-Next** for customer relationships and cross-sell leads.
- **Axis Pulse** (HRMS + intranet).
- **OMS** (Order Management) for TPP products.

## Role-Specific Benefits
- **Flagship branch allowance** — ₹10,000/month for Flagship BMs (Tier-1 metros).
- **TPP incentive** — quarterly variable, typically 8-15% of annual CTC for top quartile performance.
- **Company car** — BMs at CM band and above are eligible for the corporate lease car scheme.

## Role-Specific Leave Norms
- Branch must have coverage at all times — plan PL with your Cluster Head 2 weeks in advance so a neighbouring BM can cover.
- Weekend rotation: typically 1 Saturday / month as Branch-In-Charge for high-footfall branches.

## 30-60-90 Day Plan
- **Day 30**: Meet top 20 HNI customers, complete Finacle certification, review audit file.
- **Day 60**: First CASA + TPP target check-in; identify 2 quick-win cross-sell campaigns.
- **Day 90**: Branch business plan signed off with Cluster Head; bottom-quartile team members have PIPs or coaching plans.

## Mandatory Certifications
- **NISM-V(A)** Mutual Fund Distributor — within 90 days.
- **IRDA Corporate Agent** certification — within 60 days.
- **AML/KYC certification** (internal Axis LMS) — within 30 days.
