# Role Guide — Business Development Manager (BDM, Corporate Salary)

## Reporting & Band
- **Band**: typically DM (Deputy Manager) to SM (Senior Manager).
- **Reports to**: Area Sales Manager → Regional Sales Head (Corporate Salary).
- **Team**: individual contributor in early band; small cluster of 3-6 sales officers at SM level.

## KRAs (measured monthly)
1. **New corporate tie-ups** — number of corporates signed for salary accounts.
2. **Account activation rate** — % of issued salary accounts that become active within 60 days.
3. **Cross-sell**: credit cards, personal loans, insurance per new salary account.
4. **Portfolio quality** — attrition, dormancy, average balance.
5. **HR relationship depth** — named contact at each corporate, payroll on-time %.

## Travel Expectations
- **Heavy field role** — 70-80% of time in the market meeting HR leaders, finance heads, and admin SPOCs at corporates.
- Local conveyance reimbursed on actuals (see travel policy).
- Overnight travel within zone: ~2-4 nights per month for tier-2 corporate pitches.
- No international travel.

## Tools You'll Use Day-1
- **CRM-Next** for lead pipeline, corporate account health, cross-sell.
- **Corporate Salary Portal** for onboarding new companies, bulk account opening.
- **Axis Mobile for Business** demos during client pitches.

## Role-Specific Benefits
- **Fuel reimbursement** — ₹15,000/month (SM) against fuel card receipts.
- **Mobile bill** — ₹1,800/month SM tier.
- **Sales incentive** — monthly, uncapped, typically 15-30% of annual CTC for top performers.
- **President's Club** — annual offsite for top 10% sales performers (last three years: Phuket, Istanbul, Cape Town).

## Role-Specific Leave Norms
- Sales cycle is month-end heavy — PL during the last week of any month needs ASM approval.
- Sunday off; one working Saturday per month.

## 30-60-90 Day Plan
- **Day 30**: Territory mapping, top 50 corporate target list, shadow a senior BDM on 5 calls.
- **Day 60**: First 2-3 corporate tie-ups closed; onboard ≥50 salary accounts.
- **Day 90**: Monthly business plan signed off; 5 live corporate pipelines in active stage.

## Mandatory Certifications
- **NISM-V(A)** Mutual Fund Distributor — within 90 days.
- **IRDA Corporate Agent** — within 60 days.
- **Internal AML/KYC + Sales Code of Conduct** — within 30 days.
