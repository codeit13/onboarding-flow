# Role Guide — Relationship Manager (RM, Burgundy / Priority / Wealth)

## Reporting & Band
- **Band**: AM/DM for Priority RM; SM for Burgundy RM; CM for Burgundy Private RM.
- **Reports to**: Branch Manager or Cluster RM Head, dotted-line to Wealth Product Head.

## KRAs (measured monthly)
1. **Book growth** — AUM, deposits, investments managed.
2. **Cross-sell depth** — products per customer (target: 4+ for Burgundy, 6+ for Burgundy Private).
3. **Revenue — TPP** — mutual funds, PMS, AIF, insurance, structured products.
4. **Customer retention** — attrition %, NPS, quarterly client reviews completed.
5. **Compliance** — suitability assessments, FATCA/CRS updates, KYC refresh.

## Travel Expectations
- Mix of in-branch meetings and client visits — 30-40% out of branch time.
- For Burgundy Private RMs, frequent home/office visits to HNI clients; 3-5 overnight trips per year to top-tier client locations or product events.
- Conveyance reimbursed on actuals; company car scheme for CM-band Burgundy Private RMs.

## Tools You'll Use Day-1
- **CRM-Next** (Wealth module) — 360° client view, mandate tracking.
- **PMS Portal** — BNP Paribas / Axis AMC / ASK co-managed dashboards.
- **Hexagon** — investment advisory + portfolio analytics.
- **DeepDrive** — proprietary research platform.

## Role-Specific Benefits
- **Wealth incentive** — quarterly variable, 20-40% of annual CTC for top quartile Burgundy Private RMs.
- **Burgundy Private RM offsites** — semi-annual, family included for top performers.
- **L&D**: CFP / CFA / CWM sponsorship up to ₹1 lakh/year (approval-based).

## Mandatory Certifications
- **NISM-V(A)** — within 60 days.
- **IRDA Corporate Agent** — within 60 days.
- **NISM-XA / XB** (Investment Advisor) — within 120 days (Burgundy+ only).
- **AML/KYC + Suitability & Appropriateness** (internal) — within 30 days.
