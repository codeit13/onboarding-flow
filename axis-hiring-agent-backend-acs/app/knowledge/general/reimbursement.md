# Axis Bank — Reimbursements & Allowances

All reimbursements are processed via the Concur portal and settled with the next monthly payroll (cut-off: 20th of each month).

## Mobile & Internet
- **AM / DM**: ₹1,200/month mobile bill; ₹1,000/month broadband.
- **SM**: ₹1,800/month mobile; ₹1,500/month broadband.
- **CM / VP**: ₹2,500/month mobile; ₹2,000/month broadband.
- **SVP+**: Corporate postpaid connection + ₹3,000/month broadband.

Receipts must be in the employee's name. Submit monthly in Concur.

## Fuel / Car Lease
- SM and above are eligible for the corporate car lease scheme via ALD Automotive / Orix — tax-efficient, bank pays the EMI against a portion of the CTC.
- Fuel reimbursement against fuel cards: ₹15,000/month for SM, ₹22,000/month for CM/VP, ₹35,000/month for SVP+.
- Driver salary reimbursement: up to ₹18,000/month for CM+.

## Meal Vouchers
Sodexo / Zeta meal cards loaded monthly:
- AM/DM: ₹2,200/month
- SM: ₹2,500/month
- CM+: ₹2,500/month (statutory cap)

## Learning & Development
₹25,000/year (AM-SM) or ₹50,000/year (CM+) for external courses, certifications, professional memberships. Requires manager pre-approval.

## Health Check-up
Annual full-body health check at Apollo / Max / Fortis / SRL — 100% reimbursed once per year for employee + spouse (CM+ also covers parents).

## Relocation (for new joiners)
One-time relocation support — see your Joining Letter for the exact amount. Covers packers-and-movers, first-month accommodation, and incidentals. Claim within 60 days of joining.

Contact: hr.reimbursements@axisbank.com
