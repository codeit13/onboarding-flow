# Axis Bank — Travel Policy (FY26)

All business travel must be pre-approved in the Axis Concur portal.

## Domestic Travel — Class entitlements

| Band          | Air class | Train class | Hotel tariff (per night, ex-GST) |
|---------------|-----------|-------------|-----------------------------------|
| AM / DM       | Economy   | AC 2-tier   | ₹4,500 (Tier-1) / ₹3,500 (Tier-2) |
| SM            | Economy   | AC 1st class | ₹6,000 (Tier-1) / ₹4,500 (Tier-2) |
| CM / VP       | Economy (flexi) | AC 1st class | ₹8,500 (Tier-1) / ₹6,000 (Tier-2) |
| SVP and above | Business  | AC 1st class | ₹12,000 (Tier-1) / ₹8,500 (Tier-2) |

- **Tier-1 cities**: Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune.
- Local conveyance: actuals up to ₹2,000/day (Uber/Ola receipts) or ₹12/km for own vehicle.
- Per diem (meals & incidentals): AM/DM ₹1,000; SM ₹1,400; CM+ ₹1,800.

## International Travel
Pre-approved by Function Head + Circle Head. Economy up to SM; Premium Economy for CM/VP; Business Class for SVP+. Visa costs reimbursed on actuals.

## Branch Visits (field roles)
Relationship Managers, Branch Managers, BDMs, and Cluster Heads travelling within their assigned zone: actuals against receipts, no cap within ₹800/day for intra-city, ₹3,500/day for inter-city overnight.

## Claim Process
1. Raise travel request in Concur **before** travel.
2. Upload receipts (PDF/JPG) within 15 days of return.
3. Manager approval → Finance settles in the next payroll cycle.
