# Axis Bank — Benefits & Wellness

## Insurance
- **Group Medical Insurance (GMC)** — ₹5 lakh (AM/DM), ₹7.5 lakh (SM), ₹10 lakh (CM), ₹15 lakh (VP+) family floater. Covers spouse, up to 2 dependent children, and (CM+) parents.
- **Group Term Life (GTL)** — 3× annual CTC, paid by the bank.
- **Group Personal Accident (GPA)** — ₹25 lakh coverage, 24×7.
- Top-up medical cover up to ₹50 lakh available at discounted group rates (employee-paid).

## Retirement
- **Provident Fund (EPF)**: 12% employee + 12% employer contribution.
- **Gratuity**: as per the Payment of Gratuity Act, paid on exit after 5 years.
- **Superannuation** (CM+): 15% of basic salary, optional opt-in.
- **NPS Corporate Tier 1**: up to 10% of basic salary, tax-exempt under 80CCD(2).

## Banking Perks
- **Burgundy Private** account for SVP+; **Burgundy** for CM-VP; **Priority** for SM; **Prime** for AM-DM — all with zero-fee debit cards, preferential FD rates, and waived locker charges.
- **Employee Home Loan**: up to 85% of property value at 0.5% below Axis retail rate, max ₹2 Cr.
- **Employee Auto Loan**: 1% below retail rate, max ₹25 lakh.
- **Employee Personal Loan**: ₹10 lakh at repo + 1.5%.
- **Credit cards**: Magnus / Reserve / Atlas — annual fee waived for all Axians.

## Wellness
- **Axis Active** — subsidised gym tie-ups (Cult, Gold's Gym, Anytime Fitness).
- **Mental Health** — free counselling sessions via 1to1Help.net — confidential, 8 sessions/year per employee.
- **Doctor-on-call** — Practo Plus corporate tie-up, unlimited video consults.
- **Flexible work arrangements** for new parents and caregivers.

## Family Support
- **Crèche reimbursement**: ₹7,500/month for children below 6 years (both parents Axians: single claim).
- **Higher education scholarship** for children of Axians — up to ₹1 lakh/year based on merit.
