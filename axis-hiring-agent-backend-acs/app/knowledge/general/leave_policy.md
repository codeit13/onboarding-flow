# Axis Bank — Leave Policy (FY26)

## Annual Leave Entitlements

| Leave type        | Entitlement / year | Carry forward | Encashment |
|-------------------|--------------------|--------------|-----------|
| Privilege Leave (PL) | 30 days       | Up to 45 days | On exit / at year-end above 45 |
| Casual Leave (CL) | 8 days             | No           | No        |
| Sick Leave (SL)   | 10 days            | Up to 60 (medical emergency pool) | No |
| Maternity Leave   | 26 weeks paid      | n/a          | n/a       |
| Paternity Leave   | 10 working days (within 6 months of childbirth) | n/a | n/a |
| Adoption Leave    | 26 weeks (primary caregiver) / 10 days (secondary) | n/a | n/a |
| Bereavement Leave | 5 days             | No           | No        |
| Marriage Leave    | 5 days (once in tenure) | n/a     | n/a       |

## Public Holidays
14 paid public holidays per calendar year — mix of national, state, and optional holidays. The exact list for your circle is published in Axis Pulse every December.

## Applying for Leave
- Apply via **Axis People Connect** portal or the employee app.
- Casual/Sick: can be applied same-day.
- Privilege leave of 3+ days requires 7 days' advance notice and manager approval.
- For sick leave of 3+ consecutive days, a medical certificate is required.

## Sabbatical
Employees with 5+ years of tenure can apply for an unpaid sabbatical of 3-12 months — typically for higher education, elder care, or personal reasons. Approval is at HR Head discretion.

## Work From Home
- Hybrid norm: 3 days in office, 2 days WFH for corporate roles.
- Branch, BDM, RM roles are fully on-site (customer-facing).
- Ad-hoc WFH (1-2 days/month) can be approved by line manager.
