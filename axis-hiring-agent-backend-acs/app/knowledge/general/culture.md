# Axis Bank — Culture & Values

## Our Core Values
- **Customer Centricity** — the customer is at the heart of every decision.
- **Ethics** — we operate with the highest standards of integrity and transparency.
- **Teamwork** — we win together, across functions, geographies, and bands.
- **Ownership** — each Axian owns the outcome, not just the task.
- **Execution** — bias for action. We prefer a 90% solution shipped this week over a 100% solution next quarter.

## Ways of Working
- **Open-door policy** — every Axian, regardless of band, can walk into their skip-level manager's cabin or raise a concern via the Axis People Connect portal.
- **Dignity at work** — zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or bias. Our POSH (Prevention of Sexual Harassment) committee is active across all circles.
- **Diversity & inclusion** — we're proud of GiG Axis (women returnee programme), Axis Ability (disability inclusion), and Pride@Axis (LGBTQIA+ network).
- **Feedback culture** — quarterly 1:1s are mandatory; annual 360° feedback is part of the appraisal cycle.

## Recognition
- **Axis Stars** — monthly peer-to-peer recognition with certificates + gift vouchers.
- **Annual CEO Awards** — top performers across every band and circle.
- **Service milestones** — 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 year loyalty awards with cash honoraria and trophies.

## Communication Channels
- **Axis Pulse** — internal intranet and employee app. Announcements, policy updates, buddy chat.
- **Town Halls** — quarterly, hosted by the MD & CEO; circle town halls monthly.
- **Grievance redressal** — ombudsperson hotline for confidential issues.
