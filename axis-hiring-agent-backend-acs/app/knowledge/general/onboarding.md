# Axis Bank — Your First 30 Days

## Pre-Joining (T−14 to T−1)
- You'll receive the offer packet, background verification (BGV) forms, and document checklist from the **Onboarding Team** (onboarding@axisbank.com).
- **Documents to keep ready** (scan + original on Day 1):
  - PAN card, Aadhaar, passport-size photos (4)
  - Last 3 months' salary slips + Form 16 from previous employer
  - Relieving letter / resignation acceptance from current employer
  - All academic mark sheets (10th onwards) + degree certificates
  - Address proof (passport / voter ID / electricity bill)
  - Cancelled cheque for salary account
- **Digital BGV** is initiated in parallel — typically completed in 7-10 working days via First Advantage.

## Day 1
- Report to the location mentioned in your joining letter by 9:30 AM.
- Onboarding induction: 2 hours of policies, ethics, and IT security briefing.
- ID card, laptop (role-dependent), email, SSO credentials issued same day.
- Meet your buddy (assigned via Axis Pulse a day before your joining).

## Week 1
- Function-specific induction (Retail / Wholesale / Treasury / Ops, etc.).
- Mandatory e-learning: Code of Conduct, PMLA, KYC, Information Security, POSH — all on **Axis LMS** (learn.axisbank.com). These must be completed in 14 days.
- 1:1 with your manager to align on 30-60-90 day goals.

## Month 1
- Branch / circle visit if your role is field-facing.
- First salary credited on the last working day of the month (pro-rated from joining).
- Enrolment into GMC, GPA, GTL is automatic — you'll get the policy card by Day 20.
- Choose your flexi-benefit plan (FBP) declarations in Axis Pulse by the 15th of the month.
